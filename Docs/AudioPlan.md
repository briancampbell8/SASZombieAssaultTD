# Audio Plan

## Overview
This document defines the audio strategy for SAS Zombie Assault TD, including sound categories, asset organization, and playback rules. Audio enhances clarity, feedback, and battlefield awareness.

## Audio Categories
- UI Sounds
- Turret Sounds
- Soldier Sounds
- Enemy Sounds
- Deployable Sounds
- Premium Item Sounds
- Ambient

## Playback Rules
- Looping for continuous fire
- One-shot for explosions and alerts
- Spatial audio for battlefield immersion
- Volume ducking during boss events
