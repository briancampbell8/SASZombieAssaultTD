# Enemy Roster and Behaviors

## Overview
This roster defines every zombie type in SAS Zombie Assault TD, including internal file names, behaviors, and wave appearances. These entries guide AI logic, sprite selection, and wave pacing.

---

## Swarm Zombie (Schoolboy)
- Weak, slow, common
- Baseline enemy type
- Appears Wave 1

## Schoolboy V2
- Stronger version of the Swarm Zombie
- Higher health and damage
- Appears Wave 6

## Sprinter
- Fast but fragile
- Designed to break early defenses
- Appears Wave 3

## Runner V2
- Stronger, faster Sprinter
- Appears Wave 9

## Bloater
- Large, destructive zombie
- Breaks walls and turrets
- Spawns Worms on death
- Appears Wave 10

### Worm
- Fast, weak
- Spawns from Bloater corpses
- Dangerous in groups

## Shadow
- Phases through walls and turrets
- Fast, moderate health
- Appears Wave 15

## Mamushka
- Splits into 2 → 2 → 2 on death
- High health
- Can overwhelm defenses if not controlled

## Devastator
- Boss-tier enemy
- Summons Skeletons
- Aura destroys turrets and walls
- Appears Wave 25

### Skeleton
- Minion of Devastator
- Moderate health
- Attacks walls and turrets

## Ruin (Boss)
- Final boss of the game
- Massive health pool
- Fiery tendrils knock down turrets and soldiers
- Appears Wave 40, then 10 waves later, then 5, then every wave
