# Map Flow and Battlefield Logic

## Overview
SAS Zombie Assault TD contains eight battlefields, each with unique pathing, spawn logic, and biome tiles. These descriptions come from the original game’s battlefield text and serve as the design intent for your engine.

---

## 1. Mean Street
""""This narrow and dark street is a good place to ambush the zombies, you should also be able to get some men onto the rooftops to keep them safe.""""

### Design Notes
- Linear street path
- Rooftop tiles allow SAS soldiers
- Early-game pacing
- Tight choke points

---

## 2. Sub-Zero
""""The zombies have tracked down this human refuge camp in the mountains. It should have been safe, but it isn't. Luckily it should be easily defended... for a while.""""

### Design Notes
- Snow biome
- Wide early defense area
- Later multi-lane pressure
- Potential slow-movement tiles

---

## 3. Dead Warehouse
""""Stop the zombies from getting through this cramped warehouse to the exit, funnel them in, trap them, and destroy them.""""

### Design Notes
- Indoor warehouse biome
- Narrow corridors
- High-density waves
- Strong path manipulation potential

---

## 4. Shop Til You Drop
""""Fighting in the open is normally a poor choice when outnumbered, but this time we have no alternative. Use turrets and sandbags to guide the zombies into killzones of your own design.""""

### Design Notes
- Open floor plan
- Player-created pathing
- High turret placement freedom
- Killbox-focused gameplay

---

## 5. Killtop
""""We are at risk of being surrounded, the Zombies will come from multiple directions through this vegetated hilltop. Choose your positions carefully.""""

### Design Notes
- Multi-directional spawns
- Vegetated hilltop biome
- Elevation props
- Chaotic wave pacing

---

## 6. Touchdown
""""Chaos ensued after zombies invaded the stadium. The surviving people have exited but we need to hold off zombies here so they don't spread to the suburbs.""""

### Design Notes
- Stadium biome
- Curved paths
- Barrier props
- Mid-game difficulty spike

---

## 7. Cleanup On Aisle 13
""""Zombies are swarming into the department store from the western wall - stop them from getting past you and into the mall at all cost.""""

### Design Notes
- Grocery store biome
- Single dominant spawn direction
- Aisle-based choke points
- Strong funneling potential

---

## 8. Outbreak Mansion
""""Use the natural choke points to set up a killing field and defend this high priority position. If things go south, well, let's just say that there won't be any point in extracting you.""""

### Design Notes
- Mansion + garden biome
- Multiple natural choke points
- Final boss map (Ruin)
- High-intensity late waves
