# Premium Items (Free in Restoration)

## Cryo Grenade
- Freezes small zombies
- Slows large zombies
- Excellent crowd control tool

## Incendiary Grenade
- Burns zombies over time
- Strong against clustered enemies

## Necro Grenade
- Kills anything up to Devastators
- High-damage emergency option

## Mines
- Trigger on contact
- High burst damage
- Ideal for choke points

## Repair Kits
- Heals turrets and soldiers in AoE
- Critical for long waves

## Longbow Support
- Helicopter with miniguns and rockets
- Persists for multiple waves
- Strong sustained damage

## Typhoon Bomber
- Straight-line bombing run
- Excellent for long, straight paths

## Necro Nuke
- Massive explosion
- Lingering toxic cloud
- Deals heavy damage to Ruin
