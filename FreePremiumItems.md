# Free Premium Items (Modernized)

All premium items from the original SAS: Zombie Assault TD are fully unlocked and free in this remake.

| Item                    | Tactical Effect                                                                 |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Cryo Grenade            | Freezes small zombies and slows larger ones. Excellent for crowd control.        |
| Incendiary Grenade      | Burns zombies over time. Strong against clustered enemies and chokepoints.       |
| Necro Grenade           | Extremely powerful. Can kill most zombies up to Devastators.                     |
| Mines                   | Passive area denial. Detonates when zombies step on them.                        |
| Repair Kit              | Instantly repairs turrets and heals infantry in a small radius.                  |
| Longbow Support         | Helicopter with miniguns and rockets. Remains active for several waves.          |
| Typhoon Bomber          | Tactical airstrike. Four bombers drop explosives in a narrow line.               |
| B-52 Carpet Bombing (New) | Strategic airstrike. Wide saturation bombing lane that covers corners and bends. |
| Necro Nuke              | Massive explosion with lingering anti-necrotic cloud. Deals heavy damage to the Ruin. |

All items are available from the start. No SAS Dollars, no crates, no timers, no paywalls.
